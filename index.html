About.

I study Informatics and Computing Engineering at the University of Porto, and I like working with low-level things, close to the hardware. I’m also a member of BEST Porto, a non-profit student organisation for technology students.

Away from the keyboard I build guitar pedals, design the PCBs for them, and tinker with DSP to write effects of my own.

I like puzzles that make you hunt for the next tool, like Notpron. I’m also starting to explore computer graphics and shaders, and I’m still learning more than I know.
